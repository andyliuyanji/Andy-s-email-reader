Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-23

This app (a personal Gmail scanner for life-admin tasks) reads Gmail metadata (subject, sender, date, snippet) only with explicit user authorization via Google OAuth.

  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.
  • Email data is processed locally on the user's machine and summarized in Discord DMs / Notion pages the user already owns.
  • No analytics, no cookies, no tracking pixels.
  • Access can be revoked at any time: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
